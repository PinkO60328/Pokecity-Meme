The Story

Six chapters. One love.

Golden hour, neon nights and a partnership the whole city is chasing.

Chapter I

Her

She walked out of the XERO station with a red bandana and a smile that meant trouble. He was already waiting at her side, sunglasses on, small and yellow and completely sure of her.

Chapter II

Ride or Die

Sirens behind them, the sun in front. She leans into the turn; he holds on and never once looks back. Some people run from the city. They run through it.

Chapter III

The Promise

Under the VICE 6 billboard at golden hour, she pulls him close. No vows, no rings. Just a promise whispered into soft yellow fur: wherever this road goes, we go together.

Chapter IV

The Job

Tactical black, a sky full of money, one last job before sunrise. She takes the lead. He takes her back. Partners in crime is just another way of saying partners.

Chapter V

The City Watches

From above, Pokécity glows purple and pink, a searchlight sweeping the bay. Somewhere down there, two hearts are moving faster than the helicopters.

Chapter VI

After Midnight

The city never sleeps. She finally does, window open to the neon, her partner in crime curled up beside her. Tomorrow can wait.
